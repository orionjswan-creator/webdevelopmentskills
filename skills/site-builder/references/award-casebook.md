# Award casebook — real winners and the patterns they teach

A working catalog of awarded / award-worthy sites, each reduced to its **extractable pattern**
(technique, structure, or strategy — never content to copy). Use it during Direction (Phase 1)
to calibrate ambition, and during competitive-research as the comparison set.
**Freshness rule:** entries age fast — when using this file more than ~6 months after its last
update, run the competitive-research skill's trend-watch mode and append/replace entries.
*Last updated: 2026-07.*

## Genre exemplars (named in industry coverage as award-level)

- **Badrutt's Palace** (hotel) — storytelling + rich multimedia + genuinely useful pages
  (events, support) — luxury hospitality that still converts; awards + bookings, not either/or.
- **Elyse Residence** (real estate, Awwwards honoree) — residential development sold as
  lifestyle: video hero, subtle motion, navigation and CTAs never buried by the cinema.

## Cross-cutting lessons (what the whole catalog agrees on)

1. **One signature moment** per site — every FWA/SOTD analysis lands here. Budget it, name
   it in DIRECTION.md, execute it flawlessly; keep everything else disciplined.
2. **Fast on mid-range devices** — recent winners share "complex visuals that load fast and
   run smoothly on mid-range hardware." Spectacle that stutters loses to restraint that glides.
3. **Three.js is the default 3D engine** of current winners; Rive appears for vector motion;
   GSAP for scroll. Boring, proven tools — novel *use*, not novel stacks.
4. **Committed identity beats variety** — winners are recognizable from any single viewport
   (one accent, one type voice, one motion grammar).
5. **Utility survives the spectacle** — hospitality winners keep booking paths obvious;
   product winners keep CTAs fixed; portfolio winners keep an HTML fast-path. Juries score
   Usability at 30% and users score it at 100%.
6. **Small budgets win on personality** (Bucks Sauce pattern): voice + color + typographic
   nerve is the award formula that costs nothing to render.

## How to use an entry (worked example)

Brief: boutique hotel, Direction phase. Pull: Balmoral (restraint + serif confidence),
Badrutt's Palace (storytelling with working utility), Elyse Residence (video-lifestyle hero,
CTAs protected). Resulting direction: hospitality-place style, video hero ≤ 12s loop,
booking bar persistent, one signature moment = seasonal ambient scene switcher. What you did
NOT pull: their photos, their copy, their palette, their logo animation.
